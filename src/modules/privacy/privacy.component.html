Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

At Lifora, your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it safe.

1. What data we collect

When you create an account and use Lifora, we collect:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (stored encrypted)
  • Profile information — optional details like age, gender, height, weight, and location
  • App data — everything you log in the app: diary entries, habits, tasks, goals, fitness, finance, health data, and more
  • Usage data — basic info like when you last logged in

2. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Lifora app. Specifically:

  • To power the features you use (habit tracking, finance tracking, etc.)
  • To send you notifications you have opted into
  • To allow you to export or delete your own data
  • To maintain and improve app performance

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. Your personal information is never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data storage & security

Your data is stored securely on our servers. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. We use JWT-based authentication to keep your account secure.

While we take reasonable precautions, no system is 100% secure. We recommend using a strong, unique password for your account.

4. Your rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Export — download all your data as JSON, CSV, or PDF from Settings
  • Delete — permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings
  • Update — edit your personal information at any time from your Profile page

5. Cookies

Lifora uses only essential cookies necessary for authentication (JWT tokens stored in memory). We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

6. Children's privacy

Lifora is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
